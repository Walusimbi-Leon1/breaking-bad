# Breaking Bad: The Complete Guide to the Greatest Television Drama Ever Made

A comprehensive companion to AMC's *Breaking Bad* — covering all five seasons, sixty-two episodes, and the film *El Camino*. This book explores the characters, themes, and artistry of what many consider the greatest television series ever made.
