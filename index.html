📖 About This Book

A comprehensive companion to AMC's Breaking Bad — covering all five seasons, sixty-two episodes, and the film El Camino. From Walter White's desperate gamble to Heisenberg's empire, from Jesse Pinkman's redemption to the legacy that changed television forever — every moment is explored in depth.

This book examines the characters, themes, symbolism, writing, cinematography, and artistry of what many consider the greatest television series ever made. Complete with character studies, landmark episode deep dives, famous quotes, and the legacy of the Albuquerque universe including Better Call Saul.
